Simple tools for daily practice

Free companions for Buddhist practice, whatever your school, made to be easy for everyone, including people who don’t get on with technology.

Dharma Practice

For daily practice, from a quiet sit to the Ngöndro

Sit with a gentle meditation timer, count your mantras and prostrations, keep a practice diary, read the daily prayers, see the practice days of your tradition, and explore the refuge tree, all in one calm place.

  • MeditateA timer with bells, and a kind streak
  • CountTap to count, or add a mala at a time
  • TextsPrayers in Tibetan, phonetics and English
  • DiaryTick what you practised each day
  • CalendarAuspicious days, uposatha and the moon
  • LearnWho’s who in the refuge tree

WHY

Practice should come first, not the phone. So this is kept small and calm: open it, practise, close it. It’s free, and your counts stay on your own phone.
