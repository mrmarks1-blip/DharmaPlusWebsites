Dharma Practice

For daily practice, from a quiet sit to the Ngöndro

Everything for your daily sit and practice in one calm place. Open it, practise, and put it down again.

WHAT’S INSIDE

  • MeditateA timer with gentle bells
  • CountMantras and prostrations, a mala at a time
  • PrayersIn Tibetan, phonetics and English
  • DiaryTick off what you practised each day
  • CalendarSpecial practice days and the moon
  • LearnWho’s who in the refuge tree

WHY

Practice should come first, not the phone. So this is kept small and calm: open it, practice, close it. It’s free, and your counts stay on your own phone.
