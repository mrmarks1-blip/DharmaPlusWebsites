## "About this practice" (shown from the ⓘ button)

The guru yoga of the White A comes from the Dzogchen teachings. It traces back
to Garab Dorje, the first human teacher of Dzogchen, and has travelled through
many teachers and places, adapted along the way while keeping the same essence.

It was taught widely by Chögyal Namkhai Norbu. James Low and the Simply Being
sangha teach a simple form: sounding A three times, then resting.

In guru yoga we unite our own mind with the mind of the teacher, which is our
own open, aware nature. The white A stands for that nature.

This guide is only a reminder of the steps. Please learn the practice from a
qualified teacher.

## The guided steps (you tap to move on; no microphone)

1. **Sit comfortably.** Sit upright and relaxed, on a cushion or a chair. Let
   the breath settle by itself.
2. **The white A.** In the space in front of you, imagine a white Tibetan
   letter A (ཨ), clear and shining, inside a sphere of five-coloured rainbow
   light.
   - *With my teacher:* Imagine your teacher in the space in front of you, with
     a white letter A shining at their heart. Their mind and your own true
     nature are not two.
3. **Sound A** (×3, "Sound 1 of 3" …). Sound a long, relaxed "Aaah", aloud or
   silently. The white A shines brighter, and your mind and the A become one.
   Tap when the sound has finished.
   *(On screen: the A glows brighter while you sound it.)*
4. **Rest in openness.** The A dissolves into clear, open space, like a
   cloudless sky. Simply remain relaxed and present, without adding or changing
   anything. If you get lost in thoughts, sound A again.
   *(On screen: the A opens out into a soft sky. Buttons: "Sound A again" /
   "Finish".)*
5. **Finish**, then the dedication of merit.
