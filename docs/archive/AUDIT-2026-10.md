# App audit: where things live (4 October 2026)

This audit came from building the new tour, which walks through the whole app in 13 steps. Each step had to say plainly where something is and why it's there, and anything that was hard to explain went on this list.

## The shape of the app

| Tab | Its job | What's on it now |
|---|---|---|
| **Today** | What to do today | Words for today (on art), Wanna practise? countdown, a session in progress, the day (Tibetan date, moon, auspicious days, your plans), progress, the timer if you meditate, backup reminder |
| **Practice** | Doing it | Count a session, then dropdowns: Practise together (highlighted), Meditation timer, Kinds of meditation, Breath, Open sitting, Ngöndro, Other practices |
| **Texts** | Reading and chanting | The Seven-Line Prayer, then dropdowns: Daily prayers, Long-life prayers, Patrul Rinpoche · Garab Dorje, Smoke offerings, Other texts, Locked texts, Listen, When someone has died, Practice texts, Caring for texts |
| **Learn** | Understanding | Refuge tree, The practices, Tibetan calendar, The full moon, Tour, Generosity, About |
| **Settings** (the cog on Today) | Making it yours | Backup, your tradition and Today page, look, text size, sounds, calendar, notifications and bells, daily reminder |

Overall this structure holds up well. Each tab answers one question: *today? doing? reading? understanding?*

## Fixed in this round

1. **Practice dropdowns started open.** They now all start closed and remember what you open.
2. **The timer and Practise together** now each have their own dropdown, and Practise together is highlighted.
3. **Two "start" nudges on Today.** The new countdown card no longer shows while a counting session or a sit is already running, so only one nudge shows at a time.
4. **The full moon** now has a page in Learn, sits next to the calendar, and is linked from its notification.
5. **Kuntuzangpo** is now in the app. The 49-days section links to it directly, where it used to link out.
6. **Notifications** were just two times. They're now one place for words, nudges, reminders, the bell and full moons. Settings shows what is on.
7. **The tour** was 4 steps inside a sheet. It's now 13 steps, shown on the real pages with the part lit up, and it opens by itself after first-run setup.

## Recommendations (not done yet; your call)

1. **The Seven-Line Prayer appears twice on Texts**: once as the feature card at the top, and again in Daily prayers. Keeping both is fine (people look in the list). Or the list copy could go.
2. **Texts is long.** It has 10 dropdowns. Two possible changes:
   - Move *Listen*, *Practice texts* and *Caring for texts* under one "More" heading at the bottom.
   - Or move *Caring for texts* to Learn.
3. **"Kinds of meditation" and "Open sitting"** overlap: the Three Aa is a kind of meditation. You could merge them into one "Meditation" dropdown, with the timer at the top.
4. **The daily reminder (calendar) vs notifications.** Settings now has both:
   - "A daily reminder" puts an event in your own calendar.
   - Notifications send messages from the app.

   Both are useful, because iPhones without the app on the Home Screen can't get notifications. The labels could make the difference clearer, e.g. "A reminder in my calendar".
5. **Learn → The practices** only covers ngöndro and a few others. It could also explain the breath practices and Kinds of meditation, or link to them.
6. **Today can get long** if everything is switched on. It could show at most 4 cards and put the rest behind "More for today". This should wait until people have used the new cards for a while.
